Privacy Policy

Willow is a private personal assistant used only by its owner.

Willow accesses Google Calendar data solely to read, create, update and manage calendar events when requested by the owner. The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Authentication credentials are stored privately on the owner's systems. Access can be revoked at any time through the owner's Google Account settings.
